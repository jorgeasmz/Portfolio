## Accuracy Says Almost Nothing Here

Roughly 20% of the customers in this dataset churn. A model that predicts nobody ever leaves is
therefore right 79.6% of the time while being entirely useless, and the trained Random Forest
reaches 86.9%. Seven points of separation is the entire distance between the model and doing
nothing.

Recall on the positive class is the number that matters. At the default 0.5 threshold the model
catches 44% of the customers who actually leave. A customer who is never flagged can never be
retained, so more than half the opportunity is lost before anyone reads the output.

Sweeping the threshold against F1 moves the operating point to **0.30**, lifting detection to
62% at the cost of a longer list to work through. That trade is a business decision, and the
point of surfacing it is that 0.5 was never a decision at all.

## Smaller and Better at the Same Time

The forest was originally left unbounded, growing each tree until its leaves were pure. Measuring
depth against both quality and size:

| max_depth | ROC-AUC | PR-AUC | Artifact |
|---|---|---|---|
| None | 0.854 | 0.681 | 16.9 MB |
| 12 | 0.860 | 0.703 | 8.0 MB |
| **8** | **0.866** | **0.712** | **2.0 MB** |
| 6 | 0.861 | 0.702 | 0.8 MB |

Capping depth at 8 improves ranking quality *and* shrinks the artifact by a factor of eight.
Unbounded trees were memorising the noise in 8,000 training rows, which is what regularisation
exists to prevent. The size mattered too: the artifact is versioned in git, so every retrain was
leaving another 17 MB in the history permanently.

## The Bug That Version Drift Exposed

Encoding gender used to be guarded by `if df['Gender'].dtype == 'object'`. Under pandas 3 text
columns use a dedicated string dtype, so that check silently stopped matching, the mapping never
ran, and raw strings reached the estimator. Training failed outright with
`could not convert string to float: 'Male'`.

The serialized model had the same class of problem from the other direction: written by
scikit-learn 1.8 and loaded by 1.9, a combination the library explicitly warns can produce
invalid results without failing. Both trace back to the same root cause, which is unpinned
dependencies around a binary artifact.

## Architecture

The application reads its feature order from `model.feature_names_in_` rather than a hardcoded
list. A constant would let a retrained model and the input form drift apart silently, since
scikit-learn will happily predict on misaligned columns without raising, and the encoder that
builds a request raises if the model asks for a feature it cannot construct.
